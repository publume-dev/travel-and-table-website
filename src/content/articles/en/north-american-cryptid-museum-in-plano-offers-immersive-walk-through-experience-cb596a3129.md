The North American Cryptid Museum in Plano, Texas, is a self-paced, immersive walk-through attraction with more than 100 life-sized creatures, presented through cinematic environments, eyewitness reports, and regional folklore.

Exhibits include at least one cryptid from every U.S. state, along with legends from Canada and Mexico. Visitors can explore at their own pace, and knowledgeable storytellers are available for guided tours.
