Chef Tom Colicchio recommends three North Fork food destinations: Southold Fish Market, praised for fresh fish sourced from local fishermen with whole-fish filleting and prepared foods like chowder; Village Cheese Shop in Mattituck, a 23-year staple offering curated European and American artisan cheeses with a sit-down area for cheese plates and wine; and Peeko Oysters in New Suffolk, an oyster farm that began shipping during the pandemic, now offering freshly shucked oysters with house mignonette on a back patio by the dock. Colicchio notes he has spent summers on the North Fork for over two decades, sourcing ingredients from local producers.

These recommendations are especially useful for travelers exploring North Fork's food scene. Southold Fish Market is located at 64755 NY-25, Southold, NY, for convenient self-catering; the cheese shop at 105 Love Ln, Mattituck, is near North Fork Donut Shop and Lombardi's Italian specialty store, ideal for a food walk; Peeko Oysters at 900 1st St, New Suffolk, benefits from the Peconic Estuary's briny-sweet oysters, offering a unique tasting experience.

Colicchio's over two decades of summer visits have led him to source from local producers like 8 Hands Farm in Cutchogue, which raises animals sustainably and makes hand creams and balms. This underscores the region's farm-to-table tradition.

The guide is based solely on Colicchio's opinions and recommendations; specific operational details like current opening hours, seasonal availability, and prices are not provided. Travelers may need to check separately when planning visits.
