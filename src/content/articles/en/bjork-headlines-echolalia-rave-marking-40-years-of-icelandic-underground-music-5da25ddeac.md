To celebrate the 40th anniversary of Smekkleysa (Bad Taste), the record label organized a one-day rave called Echolalia on August 12, 2026, at Víðistaðatún sculpture park in Hafnarfjörður, Iceland, headlined by Björk.

The event highlights Smekkleysa's ethos of 'Do It Together' (DIT), emphasizing collaboration over competition, which has been central to the label since its founding in 1986 by members of The Sugarcubes and others.

As part of the celebration, Smekkleysa collaborated with Plata, a newly formed Icelandic record label launched in July 2026, to release a nine-track compilation album spanning Reykjavik's club, art pop, and experimental scenes.
