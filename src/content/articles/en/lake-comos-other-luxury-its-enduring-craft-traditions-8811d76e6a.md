A new space, Casabianca, has opened in Como town, blending modern art, dining, and accommodation: it features modern art from the De Santis collection, an all-day restaurant by Cova Milan, and three suites above the gallery.

Travelers can engage with artisan traditions at grand hotels. The Grand Hotel Tremezzo can arrange visits to the Rubelli factory, where guests can see artisans working on 18th-century looms. At Passalacqua, signature scarves by Mantero are available for purchase, featuring custom prints of the property and vignettes from its history.

These offerings reflect Lake Como’s longstanding silk and craft heritage, giving visitors direct access to traditional skills beyond typical sightseeing.
