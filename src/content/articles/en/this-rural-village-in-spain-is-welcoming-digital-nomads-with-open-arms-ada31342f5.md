Rooral, a nonprofit program in Benarraba, Spain, hosts remote workers for two weeks to two months in co-living homes and provides a co-working space, with typical group sizes of six to twelve participants.

According to Rooral, each guest injects an average of about $1,765 into the local economy per month, which helps support local businesses and the community in this Andalusian village.

Rooral sessions run in the spring and fall, and participants can stay in one of three co-living homes in the village. The program encourages participants to engage with locals through community events like Spanish lessons and potluck dinners, and participants may host their own workshops. Rooral currently operates only in Benarraba, but similar programs are emerging in Spain, such as Anceu in Galicia.
