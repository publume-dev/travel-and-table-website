According to Atlas Obscura, the 2003 photo that birthed the Backrooms legend was taken during a renovation of a former furniture store in Oshkosh; internet sleuths confirmed the match in 2024. The building today houses John's Hobbies, and the upper floor where the photo was shot now operates as an RC car racetrack.

According to Atlas Obscura, the upper floor's current use as an RC racetrack draws internet culture enthusiasts from all over the world to John's Hobbies, making the site a real-world destination for fans of the meme.

The Atlas Obscura article does not provide a specific street address, opening hours, or access details for John's Hobbies.
