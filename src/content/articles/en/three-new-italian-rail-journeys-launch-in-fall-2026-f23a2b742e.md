According to Condé Nast Traveler, the new fall lineup includes Orient Express La Dolce Vita’s Rome–Istanbul route, Belmond Venice-Simplon Orient Express’s Paris–Portofino itinerary, and FS Treni Turistici’s Milan–Alba “Truffle and Wine” route.

Travelers planning autumn 2026 trips now have concrete schedule and price anchors: the Rome–Istanbul departure runs October 22 with fares from $23,000 and stops in Venice, Budapest, and Transylvania; the Paris–Portofino train runs October 15–18, with fares from about $23,500 and a two-night stay at Splendido, A Belmond Hotel, Portofino.

For food-focused travelers, the Milan–Alba “Truffle and Wine” service operates every Sunday from October 11 through December 6, timed to the International Alba White Truffle Fair; fares start at about $80 one-way, and the restored 1970s Gran Confort carriages turn the 3.5-hour ride into a local tasting experience.
