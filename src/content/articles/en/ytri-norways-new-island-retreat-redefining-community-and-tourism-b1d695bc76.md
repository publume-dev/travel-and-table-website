Ytri Island Retreat opened in April on Husøya in the Træna archipelago, about 112 miles by ferry from Bodø. The retreat is a community-driven project, developed with local input to address the decline of the fishing industry.

The hotel has 38 rooms and suites, with interior design by Bonaparte Interiør using local materials like terrazzo made from island sand, stone, and shells. Guests can explore Sanna island, home to the Kirkhelleren cave with archaeological findings dating back at least 9,000 years.

The on-site restaurant Alma features a four-course dinner and a 12-course chef's table that reimagines a journey from Hawaii to Træna.
