The Fyllingsdalstunnelen in Bergen, Norway, opened in 2023 and is 2.9 km long. The tunnel offers a quicker alternative to going around the mountain for pedestrians and cyclists, linking two parts of the city that were previously separated.

The tunnel provides separate lanes for pedestrians and cyclists and includes a resting hall in the middle with colorful lighting that changes as you pass through, enhancing the experience for users. It connects two parts of the city previously separated by a mountain, providing a quicker passage than going around.
