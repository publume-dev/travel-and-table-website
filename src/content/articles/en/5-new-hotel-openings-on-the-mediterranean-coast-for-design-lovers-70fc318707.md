A wave of exciting hotel openings is underway along the Mediterranean coast. In Malta, Casa Bonavita opened in May with 17 rooms in an 18th-century palazzo, featuring local pottery, Sicilian marble, and Murano glass. On the French Riviera, Zannier Île de Bendor debuted in May as a 17-acre private island resort offering craft workshops.

These openings expand options for design-savvy travelers in the region. In Paros, Greece, Luura is an adults-only, all-suite hotel that recently launched with a ceramics workshop led by Maria Economides. In Menorca, Spain, Vestige Binidufà is an 11-room hideaway featuring restored 18th-century doors and local artisan craftsmanship. In Florence, Villa San Michele, a Belmond Hotel, reopened in April after an 18-month renovation incorporating Tuscan materials and a new fresco by Elena Carozzi.
