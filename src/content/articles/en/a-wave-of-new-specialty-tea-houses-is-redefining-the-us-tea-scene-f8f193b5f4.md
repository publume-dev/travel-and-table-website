Tea at Shiloh in Los Angeles is a late-night teahouse offering reserved slots from 7 to 11 p.m. with unlimited herbal tea, live jazz, and crafts like watercolors.

Han Cha, a tea salon in Chicago, opened in July and serves a selection of nine teas from Chicago-based Spirit in a high tea format with food pairings. Soft Hours, a laptop-free teahouse in Manhattan's Chinatown, opened in May, draws inspiration from the Song dynasty and offers Chinese teas without matcha or boba.

Tian Tea House opened in Kansas City, Missouri, in late 2025, serving matcha and lesser-known Thai teas, and transforms into the cocktail bar Moon at night. Heytea, a Chinese tea chain, entered the U.S. market in 2023 and now has more than 40 locations nationwide, with its first U.S. Teabar opening in June in New York.
